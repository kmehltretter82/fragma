# Common24 scoped L2 — nine profiles, 2026-09-06

The four explicit-byte Linux helpers meet the [L2 criteria](../PLAN.md) under
Alpha, hardware x86-64 and UML x86-64, with fresh acceptance also renewed for
the six earlier common24 profiles. This decision follows three completed
normal batches, independent retained-evidence/replay review and the explicit
coverage audit at Linux `b9b3e33b70b71e516930117e21de3ad2a7723747`.

Scope reviewer: Codex AI assistant, with independent AI evidence review by
`pointer_audit` and coverage review by `coverage_wave3`; not a human approval.
Support is conditional sequential-C verification for exactly these functions
under the named profiles. It is not architecture-wide verification, kernel
runtime testing, emitted-code correctness or L3. The audits and coverage
generator do not themselves award architecture levels.

## Review and runtime boundaries

The [wave-three supplement](REVIEW-WAVE3-20260906.md) independently reviews
Alpha/x86/UML type/frontend assumptions and the exact Alpha metadata and genuine
`-Os` compiler changes. The original six retain their earlier scoped reviews
and assumption pairs. All three pairs remain `implementation_proved: false`.
No approval is transferred merely because a source body or compiler is shared.

Alpha's actual positive function records `STO_ALPHA_NOPV` (`0x80`) in the
strict supported ELF container. The other admitted encoding (`0x88`) is
documented and tested, not observed on that function. This is metadata
recognition, not procedure-value, linker or ISA verification. Hardware x86 and
UML keep their genuine `-Os` commands and distinct build/header contexts.
All negative controls and source-derived assertion identities remain required.

Hardware x86's 20 L1 checks include a separate build/run of the benign
`profiles/calibration.c` model fixture under its recorded `-O2` context and
arithmetic policy. That is not common24 runtime corroboration under the genuine
`-Os` kernel command. Alpha/UML have no corresponding model-native execution.
No historical fault/trap/OOB harness is executed. Finite compiler predicates
cannot replace full-domain proofs, and none of these observations establishes L3.

Kernel callers, broader ABIs/endian variants, instrumentation, assembly, traps,
MMIO, concurrency and unverified external implementations remain outside scope.
